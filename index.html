Y 58 · DIRT

Four slots. That's the pad.

No forms, no presale, no dev wallet. One server, one coin, one vault.

SLOT 1

Paste IP

Any Java server IP. We ping it and pull its real icon, MOTD and player count.

SLOT 2

Launch coin

Its coin goes live on pump.fun. 100% of the creator fee is split to the server's vault, locked at launch.

SLOT 3

Fees fill the vault

Every trade drips creator fees in. The vault pays the hosting bill, so SOL turns into days online.

SLOT 4

Owner claims

The owner drops a claim code in the MOTD. A public ping sees it and the vault pays out to them.

Y 41 · DEEP DIRT

What's true.

Six rules. Same for every coin on the pad, and none of them can be switched off after launch.

The launcher gets 0 fees100% of the coin's creator fee goes to the server's vault. Not the launcher, not CraftPad.
Fee split locked at launchSet with pump.fun creator-fee sharing when the coin is created. Nobody can edit it after.
Vault pays hosting, or the owner after MOTD proofOnce the claim code shows up in a public ping, payouts go to the owner's wallet or straight to the host.
Unclaimed vaults keep stackingNo expiry. The fees wait until the owner shows up.
Anyone can launch for any serverThe owner still gets the money. Launching a server's coin earns the launcher nothing but the bag they buy.
Not affiliated with Mojang or MicrosoftCraftPad is independent. A server is just an IP we can ping.
